"Cash P/L to date" = money out (sales + dividends) minus money in (buys), for that symbol only — it's real whether the position is open or closed. "Total P/L" adds the live value of any shares still held, when a quantity column was available to compute a running share count and a live price could be found. Status shows "unknown" when the import didn't include a quantity column — the cash P/L is still accurate, there's just no way to tell if the position is fully closed out.
